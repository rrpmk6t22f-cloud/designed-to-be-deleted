## Context

The same country. The same subscription. A different price.

## Argument

Mozilla and Consumers International found Tinder operating up to thirty-one distinct price points for the same subscription within a single country, a spread of up to five times for identical service: $4.99 to $26.99 in the United States, $4.45 to $25.95 in the Netherlands. Across six countries, users aged thirty to forty-nine paid on average 65.3 percent more than users aged eighteen to twenty-nine. Tinder settled age-pricing claims for $17.3 million, and again for $60.5 million, the latter covering some 260,000 Californian class members under the Unruh Civil Rights Act.
