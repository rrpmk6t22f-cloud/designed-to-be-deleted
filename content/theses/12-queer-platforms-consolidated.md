## Context

Identity-specific platforms often begin because mainstream products do not adequately understand or protect a community. Their independence can be part of the trust they offer.

Acquisition brings capital, distribution and operational support, but it also relocates authority. Decisions about a queer community's infrastructure become connected to the priorities of a much larger corporate portfolio.

## Argument

HER was founded in 2013 as Dattch by Robyn Exton, built for queer women and non-binary users who had been treated as an afterthought by the mainstream products, and grew to a reported thirteen to fifteen million registered members. Match Group announced its acquisition on 20 May 2025; financial terms were not disclosed. Exton remained and stated that the team, the values and the community focus would not change. On r/actuallesbians the immediate popular response was instruction on how to request deletion of one's data.

Neither reassurance nor suspicion settles what the acquisition will produce. The important change is institutional: users who chose a specialised independent service became users of Match Group without making a new selection. Community identity remains visible at the brand level while ownership, data governance and long-term strategy consolidate above it.
